Built entirely on a phone. No laptop, no IDE — every line written, tested, and deployed from a Termux terminal on Android. The infrastructure above is real, and so is that fact.
01 — ADAPT

The first payload from a new source resolves its schema on contact. Every payload after that validates against it and evolves it in place.

02 — SCORE

Every field earns a live confidence score based on how consistently it appears. Cross the threshold, and it's auto-promoted to required.

03 — RELAY

Point any source at your own backend. Validated events are forwarded automatically, in real time, with delivery status returned inline.
